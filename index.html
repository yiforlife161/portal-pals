How to play

Click the ground to walk there (right-click works too). Click something with a sparkle to use it. Clean up junk for gold and xp, then spend it in Build mode. Each kind can only be cleared by pals of its element, shown by the badge on it, and gold is shared by all your pals.

Click the Sky Cannon for a shooting mission, the Guard Post to defend your treasure, and cross the bridges to visit the other islands. Mines and forests open in the skilling realm.
