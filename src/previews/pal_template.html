Portal Pals: character template

Draw your pal standing still, facing right. The game animates it for you.

The 3 layers

  • Back: wings, tail, legs, the back arm
  • Body: head, face, torso, armour (doesn't move)
  • Front: the weapon arm and the weapon

Rules that make it work in game

  • Face right. The weapon hand is on the right. The game mirrors the pal when it walks left.
  • Anything that should trail behind (tail, ponytail, cape) goes on the left.
  • Keep legs and arms as separate pieces so they can swing.
  • Big head, bold dark outlines, few small details: pals are about 60 px tall on screen.
  • Wings and tails may stick out of the box a bit; keep the feet on the ground line.
  • No pictures of the scene or platform, just the character.

Keep it original

  • No characters from Skylanders, Spyro or any other game, show or movie, and no look-alikes.
  • Good test: if someone could name the character it reminds them of, change the idea, not just the colours.
  • Mixing a creature + an outfit + a weapon that don't usually go together keeps it yours.
